01 Type a sentence

How it works: The model evaluates your sentence sequentially token by token. At every token step, it calculates: "Given all previous tokens, what is the exact probability of this next token appearing?"

02 Probability of each token → Perplexity

The Math: Perplexity = (1/P₁ × 1/P₂ × … × 1/Pₙ)1/N. A low probability yields a high inverse 1/P, driving perplexity upwards. Lower perplexity means the model found the text natural and unsurprising.
